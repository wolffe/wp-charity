## Getting Started

WP Charity integrates seamlessly with your organization's WordPress website, providing a complete fundraising solution. The platform allows you to:

- **Make secure donations** using WooCommerce's trusted payment system
- **Support specific campaigns** that matter to you
- **Volunteer** to help with campaign management
- **Track progress** of fundraising goals in real-time
- **Share campaigns** with your network

## Making Donations

### Quick Donation Process

1. **Browse Campaigns**: Visit any campaign page to see details about the cause
2. **Choose Donation Amount**: Select from preset amounts or enter a custom amount
3. **Complete Payment**: Use secure checkout powered by WooCommerce
4. **Receive Confirmation**: Get email confirmation and see your donation listed

### Donation Types

#### Fixed Amount Donations

- Pre-set donation amounts (e.g., $25, $50, $100)
- Quick one-click donation process
- Ideal for recurring campaigns

#### Custom Amount Donations

- Enter any amount you wish to donate
- Perfect for personalized giving
- Minimum donation amounts may apply

### Payment Security

- All payments are processed through WooCommerce
- Secure SSL encryption protects your information
- Multiple payment methods supported (cards, PayPal, etc.)

## Becoming a Volunteer

### Campaign Approval Process

#### Admin-Controlled Moderation

The website administrator controls whether campaigns require approval:

**Draft Mode (Requires Approval)**
- 📝 Campaigns are created as drafts
- 👥 Not visible to the public until approved
- ✅ Administrator reviews and publishes campaigns
- 📧 Admin receives email notifications for new campaigns

**Auto-Publish Mode (No Approval Required)**
- 🚀 Campaigns are automatically published
- 👀 Immediately visible to the public
- ⚡ No administrator review required
- 🔄 Real-time campaign creation

### How to Become a Volunteer

1. **Register Your Account**: Use the website's registration form to create your account
2. **Account Setup**: Your account will automatically be created with volunteer privileges
3. **Access Dashboard**: Log in to access the campaign management area
4. **Start Creating**: Begin creating impactful campaigns

#### Self-Registration Process

- ✅ **Open Registration**: Anyone can register for a volunteer account
- ✅ **Automatic Role Assignment**: New users automatically receive volunteer privileges
- ✅ **Immediate Access**: Start creating campaigns right after registration
- ✅ **No Manual Approval**: No need to wait for administrator approval to become a volunteer

### Volunteer Role Benefits

As a registered volunteer, you can:

- ✅ Create and manage your own campaigns
- ✅ Edit and publish campaign content
- ✅ Upload images and media
- ✅ View campaign analytics and donations
- 🚫 Cannot edit other volunteers' campaigns (for security)
- 🚫 Cannot delete other volunteers' campaigns

## Understanding Campaigns

### Campaign Types

#### Parent Campaigns

- **Main fundraising initiatives** (e.g., "Emergency Relief Fund")
- Set overall fundraising goals
- Can have multiple child campaigns
- Show combined progress from all related campaigns

#### Child Campaigns

- **Specific sub-campaigns** (e.g., "School Supplies for District A")
- Link to a parent campaign
- Contribute to the parent campaign's overall goal
- Show detailed information about specific needs

### Campaign Information

Each campaign page includes:

- **Campaign Story**: Detailed description of the cause
- **Fundraising Goal**: Target amount to be raised
- **Progress Bar**: Visual representation of current progress
- **Recent Donations**: List of recent supporters
- **Campaign Creator**: Information about who started the campaign
- **Media Content**: Photos and videos related to the cause

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is my donation secure?** A: Yes! All donations are processed through WooCommerce's secure payment system with SSL encryption.

**Q: Will I receive a receipt?** A: Yes, you'll receive an email confirmation with your donation details that can serve as a receipt.

**Q: Can I donate anonymously?** A: Donation listings show amounts and dates but don't publicly display personal information without consent.

**Q: Are donations tax-deductible?** A: This depends on your organization's tax status. Check with the organization for specific tax information.

### Donation Questions

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?** A: WooCommerce supports various payment methods including credit cards, PayPal, and other configured options.

**Q: Can I set up recurring donations?** A: This depends on the specific WooCommerce payment extensions installed by the organization.

**Q: What happens if a campaign doesn't reach its goal?** A: Donations are typically still processed and used for the stated purpose, even if the full goal isn't reached.

**Q: Can I cancel my donation?** A: Once processed, donations typically cannot be cancelled. Contact the organization directly for specific policies.

### Volunteer Questions

**Q: How do I become a volunteer?** A: Simply register for an account on the website - you'll automatically receive volunteer privileges to create campaigns.

**Q: Do I need administrator approval to become a volunteer?** A: No, registration is open and automatic. You can start creating campaigns immediately after registering.

**Q: Will my campaigns be published immediately?** A: This depends on the website's settings. Campaigns may require administrator approval (draft mode) or be published automatically (auto-publish mode).

**Q: Can I edit other people's campaigns?** A: No, volunteers can only edit campaigns they created, ensuring security and accountability.

**Q: What can I do as a volunteer?** A: Create campaigns, upload media, write content, and manage your own fundraising initiatives.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why is the Donate button disabled?** A: For variable donations, you must select all required options before the button becomes active.

**Q: The page isn't loading properly. What should I do?** A: Try refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache, or contacting the website administrator.

**Q: Can I donate from my mobile device?** A: Yes! The platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile donations.

**Q: I'm having trouble with the custom amount field.** A: Ensure you're entering a valid number above the minimum amount. The field requires at least $0.01.
